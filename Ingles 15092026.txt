Uma dica rápida:

his = dele (homem)
her = dela (mulher)
its = dele/dela para coisas ou animais
their = deles/delas
our = nosso(a)
your = seu/sua

Por isso:

Tom → his
Silvia → her
company → its
Silvia and Antonio → their
my sister and I → our.


As respostas são:

Where do you work? Where’s your office?
→ (você)
Tom works here. This is his office.
→ (dele)
The company has its office in Berlin.
→ (da empresa)
Silvia Lardi is from Italy. Her office is in Milan.
→ (dela)
Their names are Silvia and Antonio.
→ (deles/delas)
My sister and I live in São Paulo City. Our names are Rebecca and Joanne.
→ (nossos/nossas)
